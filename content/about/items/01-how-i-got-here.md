I joined CardConnect in 2014 in Product Support, then worked my way through Integration Engineer and Manager to Group Product Manager. Eleven years and a few companies later (Clover, Moov, Basys, BillingPlatform), I'm still in payments, because I never ran out of interesting problems: gateways, terminals, reconciliation, and everything that happens between a customer tapping a card and a business closing its books.
